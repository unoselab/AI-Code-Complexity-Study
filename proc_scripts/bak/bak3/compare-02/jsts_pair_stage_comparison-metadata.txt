Purpose
Compare treatment-control pair structure across paper baseline matching, our main JS/TS pairs, and our strict 1:3 JS/TS pairs.

Important interpretation
The paper uses 1:3 nearest-neighbor matching, meaning three matched control slots per treated repository. However, the same control repository can be reused across multiple treatment repositories.
