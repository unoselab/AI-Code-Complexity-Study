Purpose
Explain why paper JS/TS treatment repositories are missing from our strict 1:3 JS/TS treatment set.

Important interpretation
The paper uses 1:3 nearest-neighbor matching, meaning three matched control slots per treated repository. Our strict 1:3 pair set keeps only treatments that still have exactly three final controls after replication filtering.

This file focuses only on treatment repositories in paper_treatment_minus_strict_treatment.
